Urbn Clan - Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

Urbn Clan is a hyperlocal social app. This page says exactly what it stores, why, who else sees it, and how to get rid of it. It describes the app as it is actually built.

What you give us

  • Your email address and password, to sign you in. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and we never see them.
  • Your name, username and profile photo.
  • Your native area, district and state. These decide which clans you are placed in.
  • Your interests, which decide your public clans and the articles in your feed.
  • Your school and college names and years, if you enter them. These are optional and are used to suggest people you may already know.
  • What you post: text, photos, comments, and which posts you support.

Location

Your location is only ever read while you switch the hyperlocal radar on. It is not collected in the background, and the app does not ask for background location permission at all.

  • A radar position is stored with an expiry time and is deleted when it expires or when you go off the map.
  • Other people never receive your exact coordinates. The position shown to them is deliberately offset, and only the approximate distance is given.
  • You are only visible on the radar while you are broadcasting, and only to people who share a clan or an interest with you and are within about 100 metres.
  • Someone you have blocked never appears on your radar and you never appear on theirs.

Who can see what

  • A clan post is visible to members of that clan.
  • A circle post and a story are visible to people you have accepted as friends. Stories are deleted automatically after 24 hours.
  • Your profile name, username, photo and clan memberships are visible to other signed-in people, which is how anyone finds you.
  • Private clans are for localities and only members can see inside them. Public clans are interests and anybody can join.
  • Direct messages are visible only to the two people in the conversation. We do not read them, but they are not end-to-end encrypted: an administrator with database access could technically read them. Do not use Urbn Clan for anything that needs that protection.
  • Photos and profile pictures are stored at unguessable web addresses that are readable without signing in. Anyone you give such a link to can open it. Do not post anything that must stay private.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a device token so we can send them. It is deleted when you sign out or delete your account.

Reports and blocks

When you report something, we store what was reported, who posted it, a copy of its text at that moment, your reason, and that you were the one who reported it. The copy is kept so the report still means something if the post is later deleted. The person reported is not told who reported them. When you block someone, we store that you did; they are not notified.

Other companies involved

  • Supabase hosts the database, file storage and authentication.
  • Expo delivers push notifications.
  • News articles in your feed are fetched by your phone directly from public news feeds, which means those publishers can see your device’s IP address when an article loads. We send them nothing about you.
  • Maps on the radar screen are provided by the platform map service on your device.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show advertising, and there is no advertising or analytics tracking code in the app.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Deleting your account

Settings has a Delete my account option. It removes your profile, posts, comments, supports, photos, connections, folders, messages, radar positions and notification tokens. It is immediate and cannot be undone by you or by us. Copies may remain in encrypted backups held by our hosting provider for a short period before those backups expire.

One thing survives by design: if someone else reported your content, their report keeps the copy of what was reported. Deleting your account would otherwise erase the evidence in a complaint made against you.

Children

Urbn Clan is not for anyone under 13. If you believe a child is using it, write to us and we will remove the account.

Changes

If this changes in a way that affects what is collected or who can see it, the date at the top changes too.
