Delete your Urbn Clan account

Last updated 9 October 2026

Inside the app: Settings, then Delete my account. Type DELETE to confirm. It happens immediately.

If you cannot open the app

Email snehang24@gmail.com from the address your account uses, asking for your account to be deleted. Sending it from that address is what tells us the request is yours. We do not ask for your password, and you should never send it.

Deletion is done by hand, so allow a few days. You will get a reply confirming it.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, username, photo, location and interests
  • Your school and college details, if you entered them
  • Every post and comment you wrote, and the photos attached to them
  • Supports you gave
  • Your friend connections and folders
  • Your direct messages
  • Any radar position still held
  • Your notification token
  • Your sign-in details

It is permanent. It cannot be undone by you or by us. Copies may remain in our hosting provider's encrypted backups for a short period before those backups expire on their own.

What is kept, and why

If another person reported something you posted, their report keeps a copy of what was reported. Deleting your account would otherwise erase the evidence in a complaint made against you. Nothing else survives.

Deleting one thing instead of everything

You do not have to delete your whole account to remove a single post or comment. Both can be deleted on their own from inside the app, and your photos go with them.
