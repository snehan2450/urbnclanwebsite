Urbn Clan

Urbn Clan is a hyperlocal social app for India. It puts you in clans based on where you are from and what you are into, and lets you see people from those clans nearby.

This site exists for two things the app stores require to be readable on the web: our privacy policy, and a way to delete your account without installing the app.
